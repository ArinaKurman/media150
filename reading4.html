Reflection on “Nuclear War: The Rising Risk, and How We Stop It”

This reading was honestly very interesting because I have never seen this kind of presentation before. Usually, articles about war are written in a regular way, with just text and facts, but this one felt different. The dark red background, white text, and simple design immediately created a serious mood. What I liked the most is the contrast between the red background and the white text. The color red connects with danger, blood, war, and warning, so even before reading everything, the reader already feels that the topic is serious. The design is simple, but because of that it feels stronger. It does not distract from the message. Instead, it makes the message more dramatic. The article explains that the risk of nuclear conflict is rising, and the visual style supports this idea. In many ways, this page shows that design can change how we understand information. The topic itself is already frightening, but the way it is presented makes it feel even more real.
