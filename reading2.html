Reflection on With'Ai slop' disorting our reality

I agree with Nesrine Malik's assertion that we are gradually growing acclimated to "AI slop," or an abundance of subpar, AI-generated stuff that warps our perception of reality. The primary issue, according to Malik, is not artificial intelligence per se, but rather the way algorithms favor volume, speed, and interaction over truth or accuracy. This is particularly evident on social media, where it's become harder to determine if a video is artificial intelligence (AI)-generated or real. My own experience supports Malik's fears. AI is no longer just for fun; it is also being utilized to create political scenarios, events, and comments. This backs up Malik's warning that society is "sleepwalking" into a crisis as individuals come to accept distorted or manufactured representations of reality as normal. As a result, trust in information falls apart, making it difficult to believe that what we see is true rather than algorithmically created.
